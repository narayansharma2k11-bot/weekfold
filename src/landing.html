Editorial standards

How we choose what goes in

Sourcing
Every story names its sources: wire services, official statements and established news organisations.
Fact-check
Names, numbers and dates are checked against the original source before an edition is published.
Corrections
Mistakes are fixed in the open. Each correction is noted on the story and listed in the next edition.
Independence
No sponsor sees a story before you do. Coverage is decided by editors alone.
Balance
Facts first. Opinion, when we run it, is clearly labelled.

About us

Why Weekfold exists

News never stops, and most of it is gone by the next morning. Weekfold waits for the week to settle, then folds it into seven pages: one for each day, each holding the story that still mattered by Sunday.

It is free, global and written to be finished. Read it in one sitting and you are up to date.
