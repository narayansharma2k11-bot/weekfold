FAQ

How Weekfold works, and how we decide what goes in.

Editorial Standards

How do you source stories?
Every story names its sources: wire services, official statements and established news organisations. Each story links to at least two of them so you can check for yourself.
How are facts checked?
Names, numbers and dates are checked against the original source before an edition is published.
What happens when you get something wrong?
Mistakes are fixed in the open. Each correction is noted on the story and listed in the next edition. Send corrections to our email.
Is coverage independent?
No sponsor sees a story before you do. Coverage is decided by the editor alone. There are no ads on Weekfold.
Do you publish opinion?
Facts first. Opinion, when we run it, is clearly labelled.

General Information

What is Weekfold?
Weekfold is a free weekly news edition. News never stops, and most of it is gone by the next morning, so Weekfold waits for the week to settle and then folds it into seven pages: one for each day, each holding the stories that still mattered by Sunday.
When does the new edition come out?
Every Sunday. The whole site updates itself: a new edition replaces the current one, and last week's edition moves to Previous news.
Is it really free?
Yes. Reading is free, there are no ads and there is no paywall. The Sunday email is free too and has a one-click unsubscribe.
Where do the photos come from?
Only from Wikimedia Commons, and only public-domain or Creative Commons (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) images. Each photo is saved on Weekfold and credited to its photographer beneath the picture. We never use photos from news agencies or other news outlets. When no suitable free photo exists we show an illustration instead.
How do I get the Sunday email?
Press Subscribe at the top of any page and enter your email address. See our privacy page for what we do with it.
