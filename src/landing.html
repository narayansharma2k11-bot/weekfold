    How Weekfold is made

    1. Every story names its sourcesWire services, official statements and established news organisations, linked so you can check.
    2. Names, numbers and dates are checkedAgainst the original source before an edition goes out.
    3. Mistakes are fixed in the openCorrections are noted on the story and listed in the next edition.
    4. Independent, with no adsNo sponsor sees a story before you do. Opinion, when we run it, is labelled.

    Questions

    When does the new edition come out?
    Every Sunday. The whole site updates itself: the new edition becomes This week, and last week moves to Previous news.
    Is it really free?
    Yes. No ads, no paywall, and the Sunday email is free with a one-click unsubscribe.
    Where do the photos come from?
    Only from Wikimedia Commons, and only public-domain or Creative Commons images. Each photo is saved on Weekfold and credited to its photographer. When no free photo exists we show an illustration instead.
    How do I report a mistake?
    Email weekfoldsupport@gmail.com. Corrections are made on the story and listed in the next edition.
    How do I get the Sunday email?
    Press Subscribe at the top of any page. See the privacy page for what we do with your address.

    Why Weekfold exists

    News never stops, and most of it is gone by the next morning. Weekfold waits for the week to settle, then folds it into seven pages: one for each day, each holding the stories that still mattered by Sunday.

    It is free, global and written to be finished. Read it in one sitting and you are up to date.
