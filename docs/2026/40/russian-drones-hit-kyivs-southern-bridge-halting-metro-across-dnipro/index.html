Russian drones hit Kyiv's Southern Bridge, halting metro across Dnipro

Two drones struck near the supports of the cable-stayed bridge, the first drone attack on one of the capital's river crossings.

Two Russian drones hit the Southern (Pivdennyi) Bridge in Kyiv at around 11:30 local time on Thursday 1 October, while cars and a metro train were crossing. Footage showed an explosion near one of the columns of the cable-stayed bridge and thick black smoke rising from the structure, which carries road traffic and metro trains between the city's eastern and western banks.

Mayor Vitali Klitschko said metro and surface public transport over the bridge had been halted while engineers inspected it, and that it was the first time drones had targeted one of the capital's bridges. Dust entered the passing train through its vents, but no passengers were hurt. Traffic resumed by mid-afternoon after a preliminary check found no critical structural damage. The same night, drones hit a school in Solomianskyi district, where pupils were sheltering, and destroyed a data centre at a Pecherskyi exhibition complex.

The strike followed a warning a day earlier from Sergiy Beskrestnov, a presidential adviser, that Russia had begun using a new jet-powered drone designed to cut power pylons and steel bridge structures. Jet drones are faster and harder to shoot down than the older Shahed-type models. Ukraine's air force counted 3,177 Russian drone strikes up to 29 September, up from 2,850 in August, and Kyiv says Moscow has begun a campaign to knock out power and heating before winter.

President Volodymyr Zelensky said Russia must be held fully accountable and must face a response. Elsewhere in Ukraine, overnight attacks killed at least six people and injured 38.

Kyiv's roughly three million residents depend on the Dnipro bridges, and officials fear further attacks. Within days, Russian drones also struck the city's Northern Bridge.

Why it matters

Attacks on Kyiv's river crossings and energy grid signal a new phase of Russia's air campaign aimed at paralysing Ukraine's capital ahead of winter.
