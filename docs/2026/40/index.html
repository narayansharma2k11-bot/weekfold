Edition 40 · 28 September – 4 October 2026

The week in the world: Brazil heads to Lula-Bolsonaro runoff as the right surges

Every story from 28 September – 4 October 2026, one page for every day. Read it as a book →

Monday 28 September 2026

Conflict

Myanmar jet bombs busy Rakhine market, killing dozens of civilians

A military air strike near the main wholesale market in Kyauktaw, in rebel-held Rakhine state, killed at least 33 people, a toll that later rose to 49.

Tech

AMD buys Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion

The chipmaker is acquiring the 'world model' start-up in an all-stock deal, with its founder becoming AMD's chief scientist.

Climate

Hurricane Polo slams into Mexico's Baja California peninsula

Polo made landfall late on Monday in Baja California Sur before crossing the Gulf of California to hit Sonora and soak the US Southwest.

Tuesday 29 September 2026

World

Iran sends Hormuz reopening plan to Washington via Qatar

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met Qatari mediators in New York and said he expected a US answer within a day, while Washington insisted any deal must cover Iran's nuclear programme.

Business

US consumer confidence sinks to its lowest level since 2014

The Conference Board's index fell to 81.9 in September as fuel prices and job worries weighed on households.

Tech

Trump says top AI firms sign voluntary accord to police themselves

Leaders of Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, Nvidia and xAI agreed at the White House to internal controls and outside audits.

Wednesday 30 September 2026

Conflict

Last US troops leave Iraq, closing the anti-ISIS mission

American and coalition forces completed their departure from Erbil Air Base, ending Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq after 12 years.

Business

China threatens firm response if EU builds new anti-China trade tool

Beijing's commerce ministry warned Brussels against a proposed 'European 301' instrument modelled on US trade law.

Business

Boeing wins US Navy's $20 billion next-generation fighter contract

The F/A-XX award gives Boeing both of America's sixth-generation fighter programmes.

Thursday 1 October 2026

Conflict

Russian drones hit Kyiv's Southern Bridge, halting metro across Dnipro

Two drones struck near the supports of the cable-stayed bridge, the first drone attack on one of the capital's river crossings.

Climate

Swiss glaciers lose more than 5% of their ice in a year

Monitors say 2026 was the second-worst melt year on record, with several small glaciers vanishing entirely.

Sport

Klopp earns first Germany win as Portugal cope without Ronaldo

Germany beat Serbia 2-0 in Munich, while Portugal won 4-2 in Denmark a day after Cristiano Ronaldo left the squad.

Friday 2 October 2026

Business

US hiring nearly stalls with just 29,000 jobs in September

Payroll growth missed forecasts, earlier months were revised down by 60,000 and unemployment rose to 4.2%.

Business

G7 agrees to release up to 100 million barrels of oil

Leaders backed a four-month release of emergency reserves, front-loaded with diesel, to calm record fuel prices.

Tech

Meta opens its Muse AI agent to home-built gadgets

New open-source code lets hobbyists run Meta's personal AI agent on cheap boards such as the ESP32 and Raspberry Pi.

Saturday 3 October 2026

World

UAE calls FlyDubai cockpit axe attack an act of terrorism

A co-pilot struck the captain with the crash axe on a Dubai-Tel Aviv flight; passengers subdued him and the jet landed safely in Saudi Arabia.

World

Huge crowds march across Spain over housing crisis

Protests in Madrid and about 50 other cities followed parliament's rejection of two tenant-protection decrees.

Politics

Latvian PM's United List wins big in security-focused election

Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs' centre-right party took about 35% of the vote, while a party critics call pro-Russian came third.

Sunday 4 October 2026

Politics

Brazil heads to Lula-Bolsonaro runoff as the right surges

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro took about 47% to President Lula's 45% in the first round, setting up a 25 October runoff.

World

Merz brings €1.3 billion aid package to Kyiv amid bridge strikes

Germany's chancellor pledged military and energy aid as Russian drones hit another Kyiv bridge during his visit.

Sport

Djokovic fights back to beat top seed Zverev in Beijing

The 39-year-old came from a set down to reach the China Open semi-finals, where he faces Daniil Medvedev.
