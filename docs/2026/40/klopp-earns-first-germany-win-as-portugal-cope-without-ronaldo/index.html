Klopp earns first Germany win as Portugal cope without Ronaldo

Germany beat Serbia 2-0 in Munich, while Portugal won 4-2 in Denmark a day after Cristiano Ronaldo left the squad.

Jürgen Klopp recorded his first win as Germany coach at the third attempt, a 2-0 Nations League victory over Serbia in Munich on 1 October. Tom Bischof scored his first senior international goal after Florian Wirtz hit the post, and Wirtz added the second after the hour. Klopp had opened with a draw against the Netherlands and a defeat to Greece, and fielded an entirely changed starting line-up including two debutants.

In Copenhagen, Portugal beat Denmark 4-2 in their first game since Ronaldo walked out following an apparent rift with coach Jorge Jesus. João Cancelo, Vitinha and João Félix were among the scorers as Portugal stayed top of their group with maximum points.

Why it matters

Germany's revival under one of football's best-known coaches and Ronaldo's uncertain international future are two of European football's biggest storylines.
