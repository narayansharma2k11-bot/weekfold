AMD buys Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion

The chipmaker is acquiring the 'world model' start-up in an all-stock deal, with its founder becoming AMD's chief scientist.

AMD announced on 28 September that it will acquire World Labs, the AI start-up founded in 2024 by Stanford computer scientist Fei-Fei Li, for $8.2 billion. The deal, paid in AMD shares, is expected to close before the end of 2026 subject to regulatory approval. Li, best known for creating the ImageNet dataset, joins AMD as chief scientist reporting to chief executive Lisa Su.

World Labs builds 'world models' that try to understand physical space; its Marble product generates simulated 3D environments used in entertainment and to train robots. The price is roughly 75% above the company's valuation in a February funding round. Analysts see the purchase as a way for AMD to gain model-building credibility and compete with Nvidia, which already offers its own open world models.

Why it matters

The deal shows chipmakers moving up the AI stack into model research, and underlines the race to build simulation tools for robotics.
