Djokovic fights back to beat top seed Zverev in Beijing

The 39-year-old came from a set down to reach the China Open semi-finals, where he faces Daniil Medvedev.

Novak Djokovic beat top seed Alexander Zverev 4-6, 6-4, 6-4 in the China Open quarter-finals in Beijing on 4 October, in a match lasting about two hours and 35 minutes. After a slow start in cold conditions, the Serb broke serve late in each of the last two sets to win. Zverev, the reigning French Open and US Open champion, served 19 aces.

Djokovic, a six-time champion in Beijing who has never lost a match there, is playing the event for the first time since 2015 and is chasing his first title of 2026. He said the win showed he could still beat the best on big stages. He next meets Daniil Medvedev, who beat Francisco Cerundolo 7-6, 6-3.

Why it matters

Djokovic's late-career run against the sport's new leaders keeps one of tennis's greatest players in the title hunt.
