Vinícius sent off for hair pull as Real Madrid edge Villarreal

Real Madrid won 1-0 at the Bernabéu, but their Brazilian forward's red card could rule him out of the next Clásico.

Real Madrid beat Villarreal 1-0 in La Liga on Saturday, but Vinícius Júnior was sent off about 20 minutes after coming on as a substitute. He thought he had made it 2-0, but a VAR review ruled out the goal and found he had pulled Villarreal's Nathan Saliba's hair in the build-up. The referee also reported that he protested aggressively.

Vinícius apologised on social media to the team and fans. Spanish rules treat hair-pulling as violent conduct, which carries a one-to-three-match ban. Real's next three league games are against Sevilla, Barcelona and Racing Santander, so he could miss El Clásico.

Why it matters

Losing one of football's biggest stars for El Clásico would affect one of the most-watched club fixtures in the world.
