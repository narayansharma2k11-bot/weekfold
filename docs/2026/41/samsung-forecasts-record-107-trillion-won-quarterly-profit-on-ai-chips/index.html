Samsung forecasts record 107 trillion won quarterly profit on AI chips

Samsung Electronics expects third-quarter operating profit of about 107.4 trillion won, roughly nine times last year's level, driven by memory chips for AI.

Samsung Electronics guided on Thursday for third-quarter operating profit of about 107.4 trillion won (around $80 billion) on sales of roughly 195 trillion won. That compares with 12.17 trillion won in profit a year earlier and makes Samsung the first Korean company to top 100 trillion won in a single quarter. The profit figure slightly beat analysts' consensus, while sales came in below forecasts.

Analysts credit memory chips, with high-bandwidth memory for Nvidia's AI platforms and double-digit DRAM price rises doing the heavy lifting. The smartphone and devices division is expected to post a loss as handset demand weakens. Final results are due on 29 October.

Why it matters

The result shows how the AI boom is concentrating huge profits in memory-chip makers and squeezing makers of consumer devices.
