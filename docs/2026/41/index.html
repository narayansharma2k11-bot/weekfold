Monday 5 October 2026

Science

Optogenetics pioneers win Nobel medicine prize for light-controlled neurons

Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel share the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering a light-activated molecular switch that lets scientists turn individual nerve cells on and off.

Politics

Flávio Bolsonaro edges Lula as Brazil heads to 25 October runoff

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro finished narrowly ahead of President Lula in the first round, upsetting polls and setting up a runoff.

Politics

Spain's Sánchez calls snap election for 29 November over housing

After parliament blocked his housing decrees, Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez brought the general election forward to 29 November.

Politics

Separatist Parti Québécois wins Quebec election, falls short of majority

Paul St-Pierre Plamondon will become Quebec's premier at the head of a minority government and has promised an independence referendum later in his term.

Tuesday 6 October 2026

Science

IceCube founder Francis Halzen wins Nobel Prize in Physics

The Belgian-born physicist is honoured for leading the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole and for detecting high-energy neutrinos from deep space.

Politics

AfD lawmaker elected Saxony-Anhalt speaker, a first since 1945

Tobias Rausch has become the first far-right president of a German state parliament since the Nazi era.

World

Trump approves firing-squad execution for Fort Hood gunman Nidal Hasan

The former Army psychiatrist who killed 13 people in 2009 could become the first US service member executed since 1961.

Sport

Djokovic wins seventh China Open as injured de Minaur retires

Novak Djokovic took his 102nd career title and stayed unbeaten in Beijing at 34–0.

Wednesday 7 October 2026

Conflict

Russian missile barrage kills dozens in Ukraine, flattening Pryluky apartments

Seventy missiles and dozens of drones hit Ukraine overnight, and a strike on a five-storey building in Pryluky killed 22 people, five of them children.

Science

Kagan and Soai share chemistry Nobel for mirror-image molecule breakthrough

The French and Japanese chemists showed how reactions can be pushed to produce only one 'handed' form of a molecule.

Business

RBI raises repo rate to 5.50% in first hike since 2023

Conflict

Houthi strikes on Riyadh and Abha airports kill three

Attacks on two Saudi airports killed three foreign residents and injured more than 30, deepening the Saudi–Houthi conflict.

Business

US freezes green-card sponsorship for eight tech and IT giants

The Labor Department has stopped accepting or processing permanent labour certifications from Microsoft, Adobe and six major IT outsourcing firms, citing alleged abuse of immigration rules.

Culture

The Swedish Academy honoured the 76-year-old classicist and poet for work that reinvents ancient texts in new literary forms.

Business

Oil jumps toward $106 as Hormuz tanker attacks hit record

Brent crude climbed nearly 5% to around $105 a barrel as Iranian attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz intensified.

Tech

World
