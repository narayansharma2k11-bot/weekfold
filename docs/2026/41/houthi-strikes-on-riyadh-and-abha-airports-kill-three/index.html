Conflict · Wednesday 7 October 2026

Houthi strikes on Riyadh and Abha airports kill three

Attacks on two Saudi airports killed three foreign residents and injured more than 30, deepening the Saudi–Houthi conflict.

Saudi authorities said attacks on 6 and 7 October on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh and Abha International Airport killed three people and injured more than 30. Two women from Morocco and Algeria died in Abha, and a Sudanese man died in Riyadh. The injured included people from Saudi Arabia, Bangladesh, the Philippines, Egypt and Pakistan. The Houthis said they targeted the Riyadh airport. The same day, the group also fired missiles and drones at Aden's airport in Yemen.

The Saudi-led coalition said the attacks would not go unpunished and reported strikes on more than 80 Houthi sites. Saudi Arabia re-entered the Yemen war last month after Houthi advances along the Red Sea coast, a conflict tied to the wider US–Iran war.

Why it matters

Strikes on major Gulf airports threaten civil aviation in the region and show the Yemen war spreading further into Saudi territory.
