Hurricane Isaias slams Florida Panhandle, leaving four dead

The storm made landfall near Destin as a Category 2 hurricane, cutting power to more than 835,000 customers across the US Southeast.

Hurricane Isaias came ashore near Destin, Florida, on Friday evening local time as a Category 2 storm, after peaking at Category 3 over the Gulf of Mexico. Gusts of more than 110 mph were recorded in the western Panhandle, and storm surge flooded streets in Destin and Fort Walton Beach.

At least four people died. In Escambia County, Florida, a woman was killed when a tree fell on her home and a man died of carbon monoxide poisoning from a generator. In Alabama, one person died when a tree fell on a house in Birmingham and another in a crash with a downed tree in Barbour County.

More than 835,000 homes and businesses in Florida, Alabama and Georgia were without power on Saturday morning. By Saturday evening that had fallen to about 556,000, most of them in Florida. Isaias weakened quickly as it moved inland.

Why it matters

Late-season Gulf hurricanes can still be deadly, and the Panhandle's clean-up will take weeks for the communities hit hardest.
