Amos Kipruto storms to Chicago Marathon win in 2:01:09

The Kenyan won by nearly two minutes in a personal best, while Ethiopia's Hawi Feysa defended her women's title.

Kenya's Amos Kipruto won the 48th Chicago Marathon on Sunday in 2:01:09, a personal best that makes him joint-fifth-fastest marathoner in history. He pulled away from defending champion Jacob Kiplimo around mile 21 and finished nearly two minutes ahead of Ethiopia's Tadese Takele Bikila, with Milkesa Mengesha third and Kiplimo fourth.

Ethiopia's Hawi Feysa retained the women's title in a personal best of 2:13:33, 34 seconds ahead of Boston champion Sharon Lokedi, with Brigid Kosgei third. Switzerland's Catherine Debrunner broke her own women's wheelchair world record in 1:33:16. More than 55,000 runners took part in cool, sunny conditions.

Why it matters

Chicago is one of athletics' biggest stages, and Kipruto's time puts him among the fastest marathoners ever.
