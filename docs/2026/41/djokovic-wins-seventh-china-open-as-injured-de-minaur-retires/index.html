Sport · Tuesday 6 October 2026

Djokovic wins seventh China Open as injured de Minaur retires

Novak Djokovic took his 102nd career title and stayed unbeaten in Beijing at 34–0.

Novak Djokovic won the China Open final after Alex de Minaur retired with a groin injury. The 39-year-old Serbian won a first-set tiebreak 7-6 (3), and the Australian pulled out after winning the opening game of the second set. It was Djokovic's seventh Beijing title, his first there since 2015, and kept his perfect record at the event at 34 wins from 34 matches.

The win took Djokovic to 102 ATP titles, one behind Roger Federer. Jimmy Connors holds the Open-era record of 109. Djokovic called 2026 a challenging season marked by physical and health problems. De Minaur apologised to the crowd for not being able to continue.

Why it matters

At 39, Djokovic is still winning titles and moving closer to the all-time records of men's tennis.
