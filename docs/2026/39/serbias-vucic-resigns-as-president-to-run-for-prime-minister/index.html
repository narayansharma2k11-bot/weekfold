Serbia's Vucic resigns as president to run for prime minister

Aleksandar Vucic stepped down months before his term ends, ahead of a snap parliamentary vote on 25 October in which he seeks the premiership.

Serbian President Aleksandar Vucic announced his resignation on Sunday, months before his second and final term was due to end. His Serbian Progressive Party has nominated him for prime minister ahead of snap parliamentary elections on 25 October. Parliament speaker Ana Brnabic becomes acting president, and a presidential election must be held within 90 days.

Vucic has dominated Serbian politics for more than a decade. His government has faced mass protests since a railway station canopy collapsed in Novi Sad in November 2024, killing 16 people. The student-led anti-corruption movement is contesting the election, and recent polls suggest it could pose a serious challenge.

Why it matters

Serbia balances EU membership hopes against ties with Russia and China, so its leadership shapes stability in the Western Balkans.
