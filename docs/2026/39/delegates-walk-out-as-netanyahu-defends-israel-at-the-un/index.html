Delegates walk out as Netanyahu defends Israel at the UN

Dozens left the General Assembly hall as Israel's prime minister called genocide accusations the biggest lie of the century.

Dozens of delegates walked out of the UN General Assembly on Thursday as Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu came to the podium, following a Palestinian appeal for a collective walkout. Netanyahu mocked those leaving and rejected genocide allegations over Gaza. He also described the decision to attack Iran as one of the easiest he had made and said some leaders privately thanked Israel for it.

He attacked New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani, who had called him a war criminal. Earlier that day, Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas addressed the assembly by recorded video after the US refused him a visa for the second year running, calling for urgent progress towards a two-state solution.

Why it matters

The walkout showed Israel's deepening diplomatic isolation over Gaza and the Iran war, even as it keeps strong US backing.
