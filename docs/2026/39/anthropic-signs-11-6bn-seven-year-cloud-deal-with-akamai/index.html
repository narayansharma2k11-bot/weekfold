Anthropic signs $11.6bn seven-year cloud deal with Akamai

The AI company's commitment could grow to about $20bn and comes with a warrant for up to 5% of Akamai's stock.

Akamai Technologies said Anthropic has committed $11.6bn over seven years to use its cloud computing services, with an option for up to $9bn more. Akamai also issued Anthropic a warrant for about 7.7 million shares, roughly 5% of its stock, at $111.33 each. About 2% vests with the initial commitment and the rest as spending expands. Akamai shares jumped as much as 17% in late trading after the announcement.

CEO Tom Leighton said it was the first time Akamai had granted a warrant in a customer deal. According to Bloomberg, Akamai expects to spend about $5.5bn in capital to serve the contract, which is due to start in the second half of 2027. Some investors have voiced concern about circular AI deals.

Why it matters

The deal shows the vast sums AI developers are committing to computing capacity, reshaping the global cloud industry.
