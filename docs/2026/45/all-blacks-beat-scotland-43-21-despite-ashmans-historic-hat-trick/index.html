Sport · Saturday 7 November 2026

All Blacks beat Scotland 43-21 despite Ashman's historic hat-trick

New Zealand scored seven tries at Murrayfield to stay unbeaten against Scotland in the Nations Championship.

New Zealand beat Scotland 43-21 at Murrayfield on Saturday in the Nations Championship, collecting a bonus point and extending their long unbeaten record against the Scots. Scotland hooker Ewan Ashman scored a first-half hat-trick, the first by a Scot against the All Blacks according to Yahoo Sports' match report, and Finn Russell converted all three.

The game was close early. The All Blacks led 17-7 after tries from Wallace Sititi, Ethan de Groot and Codie Taylor, but Ashman's second and third tries, the latter from a driving maul, cut the gap to 24-21 at the break after Beauden Barrett had scored. Scotland did not score again. After captain Sione Tuipulotu was sent to the sin-bin for repeated infringements, Barrett crossed for a second time, followed by Josh Moorby and replacement hooker Asafo Aumua, who sealed the win with 13 minutes left.

Barrett's double took him to 50 Test tries, making him the second All Black to reach that mark after Will Jordan. New Zealand have won 32 of their 34 Tests against Scotland, drawing two, and the AP report said the result left them level with South Africa on 20 points in the southern hemisphere standings. South Africa beat Italy 66-7 in Turin the same day. Sources differ slightly on which All Blacks players scored the first-half tries.

Why it matters

The result keeps New Zealand in the hunt for the inaugural Nations Championship final while extending Scotland's wait for a first win over them.
