Manchester United storm back from two down to beat Roma

Matheus Cunha scored twice late on as United won 3-2 in the Champions League at Old Trafford.

Manchester United beat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford on Tuesday 3 November after trailing 2-0 early on, easing pressure on head coach Michael Carrick. The Irish News, using Press Association copy, said Matias Soule and a Donyell Malen penalty put Roma ahead, with Malen's coming after Ayden Heaven was penalised for handball.

Bruno Fernandes pulled one back in the 58th minute from a retaken penalty, after Roma keeper Devis Vasquez saved the first and Bryan Cristante was ruled to have encroached. Cunha then equalised in the 83rd minute and struck the winner three minutes later. He was sent off for a second booking after an earlier caution for celebrating in the crowd. Fans protested against the Glazer family, and tennis balls were thrown on to the pitch.

The Press Association said United had gone nine matches without a win before the game; it was their third victory of the campaign. Khaleej Times reported that Paris Saint-Germain beat Villarreal 6-0 on the same night to stay perfect and top the table. United host Aston Villa on Sunday.

Why it matters

The win gives a struggling United and their under-fire coach a reprieve in Europe and a morale boost before a league game.
