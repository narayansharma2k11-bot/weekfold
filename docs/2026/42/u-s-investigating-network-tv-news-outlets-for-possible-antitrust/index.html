U.S. investigating network TV news outlets for possible antitrust violations

The Justice Department is examining whether ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC and Fox News violated antitrust law when they briefly halted their shared White House press pool coverage in September.

The U.S. Justice Department is investigating major television news networks over their September decision to briefly suspend shared White House press pool coverage. The five networks — ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC and Fox News — temporarily halted the arrangement, raising questions about possible coordination in violation of antitrust law.

The investigation reflects ongoing tensions between the Trump administration and mainstream media outlets over coverage and access.

Why it matters

Media consolidation and coordination raise questions about press freedom and competition.
