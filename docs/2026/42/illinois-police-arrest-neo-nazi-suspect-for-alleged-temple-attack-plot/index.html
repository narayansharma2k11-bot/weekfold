Illinois police arrest neo-Nazi suspect for alleged temple attack plot

Police arrested a 19-year-old self-described neo-Nazi in Illinois for allegedly planning a mass shooting at a Hindu temple in Aurora, where his rifle reportedly jammed during a test.

A 19-year-old suspect was arrested for allegedly planning a mass shooting at a Hindu temple in Aurora, Illinois. His rifle reportedly jammed during a test firing, potentially preventing a tragedy. He faces charges of making a terrorist threat and illegal gun possession.

The case underscores ongoing threats from extremists targeting religious communities in the United States.
