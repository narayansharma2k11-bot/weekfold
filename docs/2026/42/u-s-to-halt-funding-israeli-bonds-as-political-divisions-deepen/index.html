U.S. to halt funding Israeli bonds as political divisions deepen

New York City announced it will stop investing pension funds in Israeli government bonds, marking a shift in U.S. municipal investment policy following growing anti-Israel sentiment.

New York City's pension system will divest from Israeli government bonds, a symbolic but significant move that reflects growing political pressure on the U.S. over Israel's military actions in Gaza.

The divestment decision reflects broader U.S. divisions over Middle East policy and Israel's military operations.

Why it matters

U.S. cities are reassessing financial ties to Israel as public opinion shifts on Middle East conflicts.
