Sally Rooney's publisher halts UK distribution over Palestine activism

Irish author Sally Rooney's publisher stopped supplying her books in the United Kingdom after she publicly supported Palestine Action, a group the U.K. government designated as a terrorist organization.

Sally Rooney's publisher halted distribution of her books in the U.K., citing her support for Palestine Action, which the British government categorized as a terrorist group. The move raises questions about censorship and the rights of artists to express political views.

Rooney, author of the bestselling novel Normal People, has become a prominent voice supporting Palestinian rights. The publishing decision has drawn criticism from literary figures and human rights advocates.

Why it matters

The suppression of authors based on political views threatens creative freedom and artistic expression.
