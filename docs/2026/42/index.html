Edition 42 · 12 – 18 October 2026

The week in the world: Houthis strike Saudi airport for third time in as many days

Every story from 12 – 18 October 2026, one page for every day. Read it as a book →

Monday 12 October 2026

Conflict

Houthis strike Saudi airport for third time in as many days

A Houthi missile hit the passenger terminal at Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport on Monday, killing one person and forcing operations to suspend after a strike a day earlier left at least 12 dead.

Politics

India detains thousands during anti-Modi youth protests

Delhi police arrested thousands of members of the Cockroach Janta Party during weekend demonstrations against Prime Minister Modi's government, later releasing them on conditions.

World

Migrant boat capsizes en route to Yemen, 139 dead and missing

An overcrowded boat carrying migrants from Djibouti capsized in the Red Sea on Monday, killing at least 139 people and leaving 84 missing, according to U.N. reports.

Tuesday 13 October 2026

Conflict

Iran's navy strikes tanker in Strait of Hormuz amid shipping chaos

Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed responsibility for striking a third tanker in two days in the Strait of Hormuz, as cargo ships face mounting attacks threatening global trade.

World

Houthis strike Aden presidential palace, kill five

Houthi forces attacked the presidential palace in Yemen's Aden, killing five people in a strike that narrowly missed the prime minister.

Tech

Israeli spyware targets Al Jazeera journalists via targeted ads

Researchers discovered an Israeli ad-tech firm used targeted advertisements to deliver zero-click malware to the devices of Al Jazeera journalists, granting access to phones without user action.

Wednesday 14 October 2026

World

China's Pinglu Canal opens to reshape Asian trade routes

China inaugurated the $11 billion Pinglu Canal, a 134-kilometer waterway linking southwestern China to the Beibu Gulf and Southeast Asia, shortening shipping times and reducing costs for the region's largest trade partner.

Politics

U.S. investigating network TV news outlets for possible antitrust violations

The Justice Department is examining whether ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC and Fox News violated antitrust law when they briefly halted their shared White House press pool coverage in September.

Culture

Sally Rooney's publisher halts UK distribution over Palestine activism

Irish author Sally Rooney's publisher stopped supplying her books in the United Kingdom after she publicly supported Palestine Action, a group the U.K. government designated as a terrorist organization.

Thursday 15 October 2026

Conflict

Russia intensifies Ukraine attacks as Trump claim of ceasefire falls apart

Russian drone strikes killed at least nine civilians in Ukraine on Wednesday, leaving hundreds of thousands without power. President Zelensky said he felt betrayed by Trump's announcement of an energy ceasefire neither side supports.

World

Two cargo ships sink in Strait of Hormuz as Iranian attacks escalate

Two cargo vessels sank after being struck by projectiles of unknown origin in the Strait of Hormuz, adding to a wave of attacks on commercial shipping.

Politics

U.S. to halt funding Israeli bonds as political divisions deepen

New York City announced it will stop investing pension funds in Israeli government bonds, marking a shift in U.S. municipal investment policy following growing anti-Israel sentiment.

Friday 16 October 2026

World

School shooting in Oregon kills four; teen suspect shot by community member

A 16-year-old opened fire at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon on Friday, killing three children and a teacher before an armed community member shot and killed the suspect.

World

Illinois police arrest neo-Nazi suspect for alleged temple attack plot

Police arrested a 19-year-old self-described neo-Nazi in Illinois for allegedly planning a mass shooting at a Hindu temple in Aurora, where his rifle reportedly jammed during a test.

Politics

Turkey silences independent broadcaster in fresh media crackdown

Police raided the Istanbul offices of Halk TV, one of Turkey's few remaining independent outlets, arrested staff and appointed a government trustee to oversee operations.

Saturday 17 October 2026

World

Student protests over education and governance spread across three continents

Student-led demonstrations that began in France over education policy have spread to Albania and Argentina, where protesters demand the resignation of leaders and oppose cuts to public services.

Science

U.S. balloon aircraft records breakthrough in stratospheric research

A high-altitude research balloon funded by NASA reached record heights, gathering data on atmospheric conditions and cosmic radiation.

Business

U.S. inflation rises to 3.7% as energy prices surge

The Consumer Price Index increased to 3.7% in September, driven by rising energy costs from Middle East conflicts and shipping disruptions.

Sunday 18 October 2026

World

Xenophobic violence in South Africa prompts African artists to withdraw from performances

Escalating attacks on migrants in South Africa prompted prominent African musicians to cancel or reschedule performances, including Malian artist Salif Keita and South African singer Tyla.

Health

Dendroseris neriifolia tree clones preserved as species faces extinction

Seeds from what is believed to be the last wild Dendroseris neriifolia tree, endemic to Chile's Robinson Crusoe Island, were transported to Logan Botanic Garden in Scotland as part of an international conservation effort.

World

Costa Rica opens high-security prison modeled on El Salvador facility

Costa Rica inaugurated a high-security prison facility modeled on El Salvador's controversial CECOT facility, marking an expansion of hardline crime policies in Central America.
