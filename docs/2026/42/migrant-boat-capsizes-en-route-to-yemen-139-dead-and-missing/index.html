Migrant boat capsizes en route to Yemen, 139 dead and missing

An overcrowded boat carrying migrants from Djibouti capsized in the Red Sea on Monday, killing at least 139 people and leaving 84 missing, according to U.N. reports.

An overcrowded boat carrying migrants from Djibouti to Yemen capsized in the Red Sea on Monday, killing at least 139 people and leaving 84 others missing, according to U.N. figures. The vessel was one of many used by migrants and refugees attempting to reach employment in the Arabian Peninsula and beyond.

The sinking highlights the desperation of migrants fleeing poverty and conflict across the Horn of Africa and the Middle East, who often face dangerous sea crossings and exploitation by smugglers. The U.N. has warned of a regional humanitarian crisis as conflict, displacement and economic hardship push record numbers of people to attempt perilous journeys.

Why it matters

Mass migration and the failure to address root causes of displacement continue to cost lives in the Red Sea and across global shipping routes.
