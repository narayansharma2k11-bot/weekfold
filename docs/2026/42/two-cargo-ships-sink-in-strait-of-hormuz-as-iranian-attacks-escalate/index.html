Two cargo ships sink in Strait of Hormuz as Iranian attacks escalate

Two cargo vessels sank after being struck by projectiles of unknown origin in the Strait of Hormuz, adding to a wave of attacks on commercial shipping.

Two cargo ships sank following attacks in the Strait of Hormuz on Thursday, amid escalating assaults on commercial shipping. The U.N. has urged a diplomatic deal to keep shipping flowing and prevent a food crisis in the region.

The attacks threaten global supply chains for fertilizer and other critical commodities.

Why it matters

Shipping disruptions threaten food security and agricultural production worldwide.
