India detains thousands during anti-Modi youth protests

Delhi police arrested thousands of members of the Cockroach Janta Party during weekend demonstrations against Prime Minister Modi's government, later releasing them on conditions.

Police in India's capital detained thousands of protesters on Sunday during a youth-led demonstration against Prime Minister Narendra Modi's government. The protests, organized by the Cockroach Janta Party, drew thousands despite a security lockdown in Delhi aimed at preventing large gatherings.

Most detainees were released after police imposed conditions on their release. The show of force drew international attention as one of the largest crackdowns on youth activism in India under Modi's administration. The government faces growing unrest over economic conditions and recent statements and decisions seen as favoring certain groups.

Why it matters

India's youth are increasingly challenging Modi's political dominance as economic hardship and social tensions mount.
