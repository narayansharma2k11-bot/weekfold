U.S. balloon aircraft records breakthrough in stratospheric research

A high-altitude research balloon funded by NASA reached record heights, gathering data on atmospheric conditions and cosmic radiation.

A scientific balloon funded by NASA achieved new altitude records while gathering crucial data on atmospheric conditions in the stratosphere. The balloon, which carries sensitive instruments, reached heights that allowed researchers to study cosmic radiation and other phenomena impossible to observe from the ground.

The mission contributes to climate research and our understanding of Earth's upper atmosphere.

Why it matters

Stratospheric research helps scientists understand climate change and atmospheric dynamics.
